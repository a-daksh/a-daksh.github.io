MaxLab, led by Prof. Max Simchowitz at Carnegie Mellon University, studies the foundations of robot learning, from generative policies to reinforcement learning.

- Built a MuJoCo benchmark for generative dexterous policies on a unified 22-DoF UR5e + Allegro arm-hand system
- Built a Leap Motion teleoperation pipeline with QP-based arm IK and neural finger retargeting for data collection
- Trained flow-matching behavior cloning policies on ~10 min of robot data as the base policy for online RL fine-tuning
- Raised success on a pick-and-place task from 30% to 64% with RL post-training in ~1.3k online episodes (~7 hrs)
